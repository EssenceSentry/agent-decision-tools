# Record a commitment others will rely on

Locate the existing decision or brief. Record the choice, the owner, the outcome or public guarantee, and the reason the strongest alternative lost. Give one observable example of success when callers or researchers could interpret the same words differently.

Identify what could reopen the decision and who can act on that evidence. Keep accepted requirements distinct from technical hypotheses. If a decision supersedes another, link both records so the original rationale can be recovered. A short entry in the project’s existing record is often enough.
