# Decision and handoff checks

Use the appropriate read-back when the deliverable will be used by somebody outside the current conversation.

<a id="decision-memo"></a>

## Put the decision in the first paragraph

Name the decision maker, action, timing and the choice if deferred. Give the main evidence in the population and units that matter to them. Show uncertainty, a material downside and what would reverse the recommendation. Attach enough provenance to let a reader verify a claim without replaying the whole investigation.

<a id="engineering-handoff"></a>

## Rehearse the artifact and contract together

Supply the versioned artifact or digest, entry point, minimal input and expected output, resource and permission assumptions, failure semantics, owner and known limits. Rehearse the normal consumer command in a fresh environment. Record the observation or precise unverified prerequisite.

<a id="handoff"></a>

## Write for the next reader’s first action

Identify who will receive the handoff and what they need to do next. Point to owning artifacts and revisions instead of duplicating their history. Separate completed, attempted, blocked and open work. Include one runnable check or decision with an expected observation, plus context a fresh reader cannot infer.
