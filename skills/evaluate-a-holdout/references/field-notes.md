# Testing a predictive claim

<a id="bounded-research-loop"></a>

## Run comparable trials under a cap

Freeze the editable surface, evaluator, baseline, held-out cases, resource allowance and stop rule. Score one baseline and inspect an example by hand. Change one hypothesis per disposable trial. Log prediction, cost, result and keep/discard decision including failed trials. Stop on exhaustion, evaluator drift or an immaterial gain. Confirm the winner once on unused material.

<a id="independent-recomputation"></a>

## Recompute by a route that can disagree

Freeze the original figure, denominator, time window and revision. Build a second calculation from a separate source, grain or implementation. Reconcile differences by key or intermediate total before accepting either. Two calls to the same helper preserve the same defect.
