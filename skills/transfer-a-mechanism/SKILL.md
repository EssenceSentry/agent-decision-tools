# Test whether a mechanism transfers

Describe the relationship the target needs: what information is available, which decision follows, and what constraint must hold. Search for a source system with that relationship. Map its inputs, mechanism and failure conditions to the target. A shared label alone gives little reason to expect transfer.

Choose a cheap discriminating test against the current approach. For a screening mechanism, calculate the load that reaches confirmation, check misses among rejected cases, and account for true candidates displaced by a capacity cap. For a library, try the awkward real caller before adopting its interface.

Report the source, the transferable property, where it breaks, and what the test would change. [Examples of mechanism mapping](references/field-notes.md)
