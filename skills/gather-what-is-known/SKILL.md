# Find what the work can rely on

Name the fact or contract you need to establish and where it is likely to live. Follow the real consumer to the public definition, or follow a reported claim to its original evidence. Check the revision and scope that apply to this user.

For suspected reuse, exercise the awkward input, ordering, early exit or failure that a duplicate might miss. The optional [Python usage map](scripts/usage_map.py) traces imports and re-exports without executing consumer code. For a factual claim, distinguish independent observations from sources repeating the same measurement.

Return the usable entry point or finding, its locator and any consequential limit. Stop when another search would leave the action unchanged. [Worked source note](references/source-note.md) · [Search techniques](references/field-notes.md)
