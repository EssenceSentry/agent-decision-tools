# Resolve a consequential ambiguity

Work from the requested action, its beneficiary and the observable result. Check the contract or real caller that makes a condition binding. Distinguish a stakeholder trade-off from a technical hypothesis that can be tested.

Compare the request as stated with the smallest credible alternative on the same outcome, cost and constraints. For example, the cadence of an update can differ from the cadence of a required status report. A binding term stays in force until its owner changes it.

Ask the owner only when the unresolved trade changes the deliverable. Otherwise proceed with the supported interpretation and make a consequential assumption visible. [Ways to reframe a request](references/field-notes.md)
